02Guide, not spy

Summaries, not surveillance.

Kite pays attention to patterns: how much, when, and what kind. Then it tells you what matters in plain English. If something serious ever comes up, you’ll hear about it right away, with clear next steps.

You’ll hear about

  • How much time, and when
  • What they’re into lately
  • Changes worth a conversation
  • Anything genuinely risky

You won’t see

  • Their messages
  • Every search and video
  • A minute-by-minute log
  • Anything “just in case”

03Grows with them

A dial, not a switch.

Most parental controls are either on or off. Kite loosens its grip a little each year, so the day they get the whole internet isn’t a cliff. It’s just the next step.
